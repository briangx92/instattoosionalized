One upload feature, two kinds of photos, 5 ways

Each example handles both jobs: regular batch uploads for the gallery, and before and after pairs with captions. Every drop box also takes drag and drop from a computer, and in examples 2 and 4 you can drag photos into place. Try them with real photos. Nothing saves for real.

1 · A switch at the top

The uploader asks one question first: regular photos, or a before and after set? The box below changes to match. This is how most pro apps do it, one door, clear sign.
