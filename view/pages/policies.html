Shop policies

What to know before you book, and how to look after your new work.

Questions

Do you do piercings?

Yes, tattoos and piercings both. Send a request or call the shop and they will take it from there.

What should I send with my request?

Your idea, where it goes, roughly how big, and reference photos if you have them. The request form on the main page walks you through it.

Where is the shop?

1852 Main St, Northampton, PA. The shop's number is (610) 440-2700.

How do I book an appointment?

Send a request through the form on this site and pick your artist. The shop confirms every appointment itself. The shop's deposit is $40 and holds your spot.

How much is the deposit?

The shop's deposit is $40 and holds your spot.

What are your hours?

Tuesday to Saturday, 12 to 5. Artists keep their own schedules, so call ahead to be sure your artist is in.

Do you take walk-ins?

Walk-ins are welcome when an artist is free. Booking ahead is the sure way to get a spot.

What does a tattoo cost?

The shop minimum is $60. A small flash tattoo is $80. Final price depends on size and detail, and your artist confirms it before you book.

What does a piercing cost?

A standard body piercing is $45.
