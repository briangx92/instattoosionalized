The artist page: Portfolio only, with one Upload button

Your simpler layout. The artist page stays exactly as it is. Under it there is one list, the Portfolio, which is what appears on the site and the only place captions change. The Upload button offers two jobs: Gallery photos, or a Before / after pair. Each opens its own workspace, and Done brings you back to the Portfolio, where finished work already shows. Sections are still the pages inside Portfolio: flip with the pager, sort inside a section.
